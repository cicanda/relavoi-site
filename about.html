About Relavoi

Nigeria's platforms deserve privacy infrastructure built here.

Our story

No local provider existed. So we built one.

Every day across Lagos, couriers, drivers and sellers exchange personal phone numbers with strangers. Numbers get saved, reused, and sometimes abused, and platforms lose customers to off-app deals.

Nigeria has no dedicated number-masking provider. The global ones weren't built for Nigerian carriers, naira billing, or NDPR. Relavoi is. We run over local PSTN so any handset works, allocate numbers by region, and meter usage in naira.

Relavoi is built by CICANDA Ltd, headquartered in Lagos.

What we believe

Principles we ship by.

Privacy is the default

Real numbers are encrypted at rest under per-tenant keys and never returned by the API, not even to you. Lookups run on salted hashes, and plaintext numbers never reach a log line.

Reliability beats features

A missed call is a missed delivery. We'd rather ship less and page less.

Local first

Nigerian carriers, Nigerian compliance, naira pricing. Then the rest of the continent.

Where we are

Early, and honest about it.

Relavoi is in private beta. Here's what's live, what's next, and what's still on paper.

Live

Voice and SMS masking, session lifecycle, atomic number allocation, signed webhooks, WebSocket events, usage metering, tenant dashboard and operator console.

In progress

SDK hardening across iOS, Android and Flutter. Self-service onboarding. Automated number provisioning and pool auto-scaling.

On the roadmap

Secondary-carrier failover pools, encrypted recording storage, WhatsApp masking, USSD fallback, and expansion beyond Nigeria.
