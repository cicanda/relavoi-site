Webhooks

Every event, HMAC-signed.

Verify X-Relavoi-Signature against your webhook secret. It's an HMAC of timestamp.body. Dedupe on X-Relavoi-Delivery and respond 200. We retry with exponential backoff and log every attempt to your dashboard.
