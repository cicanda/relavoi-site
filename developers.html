Webhooks

Every event, HMAC-signed.

Verify X-Relavoi-Signature against your webhook secret. It's an HMAC of timestamp.body. Dedupe on X-Relavoi-Delivery and respond 200. Reject anything whose X-Relavoi-Timestamp is more than five minutes from your own clock, so a leaked payload cannot be replayed at you later. We retry with exponential backoff and log every attempt to your dashboard.

That signature is on the webhooks Relavoi sends you. Carrier callbacks travel the other way, into Relavoi, and securing them is our job. You never handle those.
