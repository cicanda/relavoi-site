Why Relavoi

Privacy isn't a feature. It's the product.

Every call between your agents and customers routes through a temporary proxy number. When the job ends, the connection disappears.

🛡

Stop off-platform leakage

Drivers and customers can't take the relationship — or the revenue — off your app once the session expires.

⚡

No app on the customer's side

Calls run over Nigerian PSTN, so they reach any handset — smartphone or feature phone. No VoIP client, no data plan, nothing to install.

§

Built for NDPR

Phone numbers AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest under per-tenant keys, never written to logs in plaintext, and a consent prompt the platform refuses to let you skip when recording is on.
