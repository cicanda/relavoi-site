Products

One privacy layer. Every channel your users talk on.

Voice and SMS masking, mobile SDKs, and the analytics to prove it's working.

01 · Voice masking

Bidirectional proxy calling over PSTN

Both parties dial a temporary proxy. Relavoi identifies the caller and bridges to the other side — each sees only the proxy.

  • Region-scoped number pools (Lagos today)
  • Configurable session cap and grace period, per tenant or per session
  • Recording gated behind an enforced NDPR consent prompt
  • Expired-session behaviour: dead line, redirect to support, or custom message
  • One-way modes when you only want A→B or B→A to connect
Product visual · voice bridge diagram
02 · SMS masking

Texts through the same proxy

"I'm at the gate" works too. SMS routes through the session's proxy number with delivery status in your webhook stream.

  • Same session, same proxy, same audit trail
  • Delivery status via sms.sent webhooks
  • Message bodies encrypted at rest, metered per message
  • Inbound SMS is not billed
Product visual · masked SMS thread
03 · Mobile SDKs

Verified calls your customers trust

SDKs for iOS, Android and Flutter add the trust layer PSTN can't: branded push alerts, in-call verification, and post-call feedback.

  • In-call verification UI — green when the call is genuinely yours, red when it isn't
  • iOS Live Activities for the in-progress call
  • Branded push: "Your courier is calling"
  • Post-call rating and issue-report components
Product visual · iOS + Android verification screens
04 · Analytics

See every call. Store no numbers.

Call volume, answer rates, session outcomes and pool utilisation — per tenant, filterable, and metered for billing.

  • Usage summaries that drive your invoice
  • Call analytics with date and status filters
  • Live pool utilisation and number health
  • Prometheus metrics endpoint for your own dashboards
